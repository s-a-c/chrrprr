# Requirements Quality Checklist

**Purpose**: Validate that requirements themselves are complete, clear, consistent, measurable, and well-structured
**Created**: 2025-12-22
**Feature**: General Requirements Quality Validation

**Note**: This checklist validates the QUALITY OF REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENTATION, not requirement implementation.

## Requirement Completeness

- [ ] CHK001 - Are all functional requirements identified and documented? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK002 - Are all non-functional requirements identified and documented? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK003 - Are user stories and acceptance criteria complete? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK004 - Are business rules and constraints documented? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK005 - Are dependencies and assumptions documented? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK006 - Are success criteria defined for all requirements? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK007 - Are edge cases and exception flows documented? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK008 - Are requirements traceable to business objectives? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK009 - Are requirements prioritized? [Completeness, Gap]
- [ ] CHK010 - Are requirements versioned and change history maintained? [Completeness, Gap]

## Requirement Clarity

- [ ] CHK011 - Are requirements written in clear, unambiguous language? [Clarity]
- [ ] CHK012 - Are technical terms defined in a glossary? [Clarity]
- [ ] CHK013 - Are requirements specific and measurable? [Clarity, Measurability]
- [ ] CHK014 - Are requirements written from user/business perspective? [Clarity]
- [ ] CHK015 - Are requirements free of implementation details? [Clarity]
- [ ] CHK016 - Are vague terms (fast, easy, user-friendly) quantified? [Clarity, Ambiguity]
- [ ] CHK017 - Are requirements structured with consistent format? [Clarity]

## Requirement Consistency

- [ ] CHK018 - Are requirements consistent with each other? [Consistency]
- [ ] CHK019 - Are requirements consistent with business objectives? [Consistency]
- [ ] CHK020 - Are requirements consistent with architecture decisions? [Consistency]
- [ ] CHK021 - Are requirements consistent with existing system capabilities? [Consistency]

## Acceptance Criteria Quality

- [ ] CHK022 - Are acceptance criteria defined for all requirements? [Acceptance Criteria]
- [ ] CHK023 - Are acceptance criteria testable and measurable? [Measurability]
- [ ] CHK024 - Are acceptance criteria written from user perspective? [Acceptance Criteria]
- [ ] CHK025 - Are acceptance criteria specific and unambiguous? [Acceptance Criteria]

## Scenario Coverage

- [ ] CHK026 - Are requirements defined for primary user flows? [Coverage, Primary Flow]
- [ ] CHK027 - Are requirements defined for alternate user flows? [Coverage, Alternate Flow]
- [ ] CHK028 - Are requirements defined for error/exception flows? [Coverage, Exception Flow]
- [ ] CHK029 - Are requirements defined for recovery flows? [Coverage, Gap]
- [ ] CHK030 - Are requirements defined for edge cases? [Coverage, Edge Case]

## Edge Case Coverage

- [ ] CHK031 - Are requirements defined for boundary conditions? [Edge Case, Gap]
- [ ] CHK032 - Are requirements defined for empty/null data scenarios? [Edge Case, Gap]
- [ ] CHK033 - Are requirements defined for maximum/minimum values? [Edge Case, Gap]
- [ ] CHK034 - Are requirements defined for concurrent operations? [Edge Case, Gap]
- [ ] CHK035 - Are requirements defined for system failures? [Edge Case, Gap]

## Non-Functional Requirements

- [ ] CHK036 - Are performance requirements specified and quantified? [NFR, Gap]
- [ ] CHK037 - Are security requirements specified? [NFR, Gap]
- [ ] CHK038 - Are accessibility requirements specified? [NFR, Gap]
- [ ] CHK039 - Are usability requirements specified? [NFR, Gap]
- [ ] CHK040 - Are scalability requirements specified? [NFR, Gap]

## Dependencies & Assumptions

- [ ] CHK041 - Are all dependencies explicitly documented? [Dependency, Gap]
- [ ] CHK042 - Are all assumptions explicitly documented? [Assumption]
- [ ] CHK043 - Are dependencies validated and confirmed? [Dependency]
- [ ] CHK044 - Are assumptions validated and confirmed? [Assumption]

## Ambiguities & Conflicts

- [ ] CHK045 - Are ambiguous terms identified and clarified? [Ambiguity]
- [ ] CHK046 - Are conflicting requirements identified and resolved? [Conflict]
- [ ] CHK047 - Are requirements reviewed for clarity and completeness? [Completeness]
- [ ] CHK048 - Are requirements validated with stakeholders? [Traceability, Gap]
